Important Health Information

Health Warning

VapeVault UK is committed to responsible retailing. Please read this information carefully.

Nicotine Addiction

Nicotine is highly addictive. Products containing nicotine should only be purchased and used by existing adult smokers or vapers. If you have never smoked, do not start vaping.

Not for Young People

Vaping products are not suitable for anyone under the age of 18. The nicotine in e-cigarettes can harm adolescent brain development. It is illegal to sell these products to anyone under 18 in the United Kingdom.

Pregnancy

Do not use nicotine products during pregnancy. Nicotine can harm foetal development. If you are pregnant and trying to quit smoking, please consult your midwife or GP for safer alternatives.

Medical Conditions

Consult your doctor before using nicotine products if you have or have had: heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, kidney or liver disease, or any other serious medical condition. Nicotine increases heart rate and blood pressure.

Vaping vs Smoking

Public Health England (now UKHSA) has stated that vaping is approximately 95% less harmful than smoking combustible cigarettes. However, vaping is not risk-free, and the long-term effects are still being studied. Vaping is intended as a tool to help smokers quit — not as a lifestyle product for non-smokers.

Getting Help to Quit

If you want to quit nicotine entirely, free support is available via the NHS Stop Smoking service: visit nhs.uk/live-well/quit-smoking or call 0300 123 1044.
